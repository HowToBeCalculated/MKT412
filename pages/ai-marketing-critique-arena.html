Instructor notes / adapt the critique rubric

The brand briefs, campaign copy, evidence constraints, and answer keys are fictional, hand-authored teaching examples of AI-style marketing drafts. This page does not call an AI model, detect AI authorship, check facts automatically, or offer a legal compliance review.

The rubric rewards matching issues to the supplied brief, choosing the stronger starting point, and avoiding unsupported accusations. Each case earns one point for the campaign choice plus one for each correctly identified issue; each extra issue selected subtracts one issue point, down to zero issue points per case. A campaign-choice point is never removed by an extra issue.

Students can disagree with the suggested creative direction if they cite the brief. Extension ideas: add new briefs, have students write their own revision, or replace the checkbox rubric with an instructor discussion.
